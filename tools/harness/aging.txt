# Aging (phase 10): a crate and boards at home, then three lives end. The
# crate goes Intact, Damaged, Breaking, gone; the boards lose a quarter of a
# board each life; lives of forty days leave the districts worse than when
# the world was made. Run it twice: the [Aging] lines are the same.
# Run in a fresh directory (it writes saves):
#   dead_pixel_survival.exe --script tools/harness/aging.txt --hidden --world-seed 1234 --shots shots/aging

survivors gone
newrun
god on
waits 1
give Saw
give Hammer
give Wood Planks 10
give Nails 16
goto home
waits 0.3
build crate
waits 13
open
waits 0.3
give Canned Food 2
give Water Bottle 2
give Rice 1
stash Canned Food 2
stash Water Bottle 2
stash Rice 1
tap Escape
goto door
waits 0.3
tap R
waits 4.5
tap R
waits 4.5
tap R
waits 4.5
aging
goto home

goto door
