# Life after day 3 (phase 13): the standing aim, chosen at each dawn from
# the world's facts. Day 4 with no rain collector: "Water that refills";
# building one finishes it, and the next dawn brings the next aim; a horde
# day with the house closed up: "Tonight". Four aims shot at 1x and 2x (the
# panel must fit the top-right), and the day length: 48 minutes a day runs
# the clock at half speed.
# Run in a fresh directory:
#   dead_pixel_survival.exe --script tools/harness/aims.txt --hidden --world-seed 1234 --shots shots/aims

newrun
god on
slay 40
waits 1
aim
expect aim none
# Day 4: the first days' plans are over.
day 4
hours 8
waits 1
aim
expect aim water
expect daycard Nobody is coming
shot 01_aim_water_1x
screen 1920 1080
uisize 2
waits 0.3
shot 02_aim_water_2x
uisize 1
screen 1280 720
waits 0.3

# A rain collector built just outside the door: the aim is met.
goto home
goto door outside
give Hammer
give Scrap Metal 2
give Wood Planks 1
build rain
waits 14
aim
expect aim water
expect aimdone
shot 03_aim_water_done
