# Boards: a hammer and planks nail a closed door shut. Nobody opens it, the
# dead split the boards before they reach the door, and boards are remembered
# by checkpoints and by the next life.
# Run in a fresh directory:
#   dead_pixel_survival.exe --script tools/harness/boards.txt --hidden --world-seed 1234 --shots shots/boards

newrun
god on
waits 1
goto door
waits 0.3
zoom 3
# No hammer yet: nothing happens.
tap R
waits 0.3
doors
give Hammer
give Wood Planks 3
tap R
waits 0.3
tap R
waits 0.3
tap R
waits 0.3
# Nailed shut: E does not open it.
tap E
waits 0.3
doors
shot 01_boarded_inside
goto door outside
waits 0.3
shot 02_boarded_outside
goto door
waits 0.3
save
waits 0.5
resume
waits 1
doors
# The dead come for you; the boards give way first.
zombie 6 0
zombie -6 0
zombie 0 6
zombie 0 -6
waits 8
doors
shot 03_splintering
kill boards test
waits 6
newrun
waits 1
doors
quit
