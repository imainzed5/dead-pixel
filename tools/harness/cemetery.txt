# Cemetery (phase 10): the next life takes a marker from a grave with no
# pack, carries it to the cemetery and sets it (F). The lead moves to the
# cemetery, depression falls once, and the stone reads the name after a
# reload. A dead survivor is buried by hand.
# The life before ends on day 11: the next one's card says how long.
# Run in a fresh directory (it writes saves):
#   dead_pixel_survival.exe --script tools/harness/cemetery.txt --hidden --world-seed 1234 --shots shots/cemetery

survivors gone
newrun

pack empty

kill Overrun on the stairs

newrun
god on
slay 40

goto grave
waits 0.3
tap E
waits 0.3
expect reader open
shot 01_reader_marker
tap E

expect marker 1
tap J
waits 0.2

shot 02_cemetery
