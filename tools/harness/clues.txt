# The way out's clues (phase 9): the first life of a world on the way out.
# A survivor tells what they heard; the note in the start home's kitchen;
# the gate seen from 15 tiles; the box read; the hut's log book; the
# radio's looped voice; the notebook's second page (Right) and the town map.
# Run in a fresh directory (it writes saves):
#   dead_pixel_survival.exe --script tools/harness/clues.txt --hidden --world-seed 1234 --shots shots/clues

campaign on
newrun
god on
hours 10
waits 1
survivors gone
slay 200
expect objective Find out why nobody has left
expect readables 3
clues

# Someone met says what they heard (trust 10 or more).
survivor house open trust 30
goto survivor
waits 0.5
talk
talk hello
expect lead 1
survivors gone

# The note on the start home's fridge: read once a life.
goto clue home
waits 0.3
expect prompt note on the fridge
tap E
waits 2.5
expect message sealed the town
shot 01_fridge_note
expect objective Find the service gate

# The radio, with a battery: three lines, then lead 1 (already known).
give Radio
give Battery
use battery
use radio
waits 8
expect message Radio:
waits 42
clues

# The gate seen from inside 15 tiles: lead 2.
goto gate
waits 1
expect lead 2
shot 02_the_gate
# The box: what it needs, and lead 3.
expect prompt Needs: fuse
tap E
waits 0.5
expect lead 3
expect objective Find a gate fuse
shot 03_gate_box

# The hut's log book: the Sergeant (lead 4).
goto clue log_book
waits 0.3
tap E
waits 2.5
expect lead 4
expect message Sgt.
shot 04_log_book

# The notebook, then its second page.
tap J
waits 0.4
shot 05_notebook
tap Right
waits 0.4
shot 06_way_out_page
tap J
waits 0.2
tap M
waits 0.4
shot 07_town_map
tap M
waits 0.2
expect leads 4
clues
quit
