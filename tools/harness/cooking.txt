# Food and water after the taps (phase 6): Dirty Water boiled in a pot at a
# campfire is a Water Bottle; Rice cooks with water; raw meat makes you ill,
# cooked meat does not. E at a burning campfire opens the Make panel's FOOD
# tab; X there feeds it.
# Run in a fresh directory:
#   dead_pixel_survival.exe --script tools/harness/cooking.txt --hidden --world-seed 1234 --shots shots/cooking

newrun
god on
waits 1
slay 300
hours 12
goto pavement
waits 0.3
give Wood Planks 2
build campfire
waits 6
light
waits 0.3
expect prompt E: Cook
give Cooking Pot
give Dirty Water 2
give Rice
give Raw Meat 2
tap E
waits 0.4
shot 01_food_tab
# Dirty water boiled: two clean bottles.
recipe Boiled Water
waits 11
recipe Boiled Water
waits 11
expect pack Water Bottle 2
# Rice with one of them, in the pot.
recipe Cooked Rice
waits 13
expect pack Cooked Rice 1
shot 02_cooked
# One piece of meat cooked; the other eaten raw.
recipe Cooked Meat
waits 9
expect pack Cooked Meat 1
tap Escape
waits 0.3
set illness 0
use cooked_meat
waits 0.5
expect need illness below 1
use rat_meat
waits 0.5
expect need illness above 15
shot 03_raw_meat
# Dirty water drunk as it is: thirst eased, illness up.
set illness 0
set thirst 40
give Dirty Water
use dirty_water
waits 0.5
expect need illness above 8
expect need thirst above 50
# The campfire fed with X.
give Wood Planks 2
tap X
waits 0.3
expect message feed the fire
expect alive
quit
