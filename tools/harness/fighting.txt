# Fighting (phase 3): the shove, the stomp on one that is down, the
# take-down, being held and tearing free, and a heavy blow that puts one on
# the floor.
# Run in a fresh directory:
#   dead_pixel_survival.exe --script tools/harness/fighting.txt --hidden --world-seed 1234 --shots shots/fighting

newrun
god on
waits 1
zoom 2.5
# Aim east (down-right on screen).
mouse 760 420

# The shove: Space throws one back and breaks the lunge it was winding up.
zombie 1 0 lunging
expect lunging 1
shove
wait 9
shot shove
expect lunging 0
expect free
waits 0.3
slay 6
waits 0.5

# One already hurt goes off its feet.
zombie 1 0 hp 15
waits 0.2
shove
waits 0.3
expect down 1
shot down
slay 6
waits 0.5

# Shoved from behind, a whole one falls too.
zombie 1 0 away
waits 0.1
shove
waits 0.3
expect down 1
shot shove_behind
slay 6
waits 0.5

# The stomp: a bat brought down on one on the floor ends it in one blow.
# (The kill count includes the three slain above: slay counts as yours.)
give Baseball Bat
select Baseball Bat
expect kills 3
zombie 1 0 down close hp 50
waits 0.1
click left
waits 0.6
expect kills 4
shot stomp
slay 6
waits 0.5

# The take-down: right click takes hold of one on the floor (or turned
# away), left click ends it.
zombie 1 0 down hp 200
waits 0.2
click right
waits 0.4
shot takedown_hold
click left
waits 1
expect kills 5
shot takedown
slay 6
waits 1

# Grabbed: one walking up to a player standing still takes hold within a few
# lunges (here, from the front, where 55 % of lunges claw instead; which ones
# do depends on the zombie, so the script waits for the hold).
zombie 2 0
waitfor 10 held
expect held
shot held
# Four quick taps of Space tear you free.
shove
wait 6
shove
wait 6
shove
wait 6
shove
wait 2
expect free
shot torn_free
slay 6
waits 1.5

# A heavy blow: the sledgehammer puts a full-health one down on any hit
# (300 health, so the blow cannot simply kill it).
give Sledgehammer
select Sledgehammer
zombie 1 0 hp 300
waits 0.1
click left
waits 0.7
expect down 1
shot sledge_down
waits 3
expect alive
quit
