# The way out across lives (phase 9). Life 1 reads the fridge note, sees
# the gate, fits the fuse and dies with a notebook. Life 2 finds the card
# line, takes the notebook from the grave's pack (its leads come with it),
# finds the fuse as life 1 left it, takes the key from the Sergeant, works
# the generator loose (the know-how set directly), pours four cans (two
# trips: two cans are as much as a pack holds), starts
# the motor and dies during the stand with the key in the pack. Life 3
# finds the fuse fitted, the fuel as the stand left it (a can burned) and
# the key in life 2's grave; the gate has closed again. It tops the fuel
# up, holds the gate with a companion beside it and both go out. The title
# card counts it; life 4 finds the gate open on day 1, the marker names who
# left, and it walks out.
# The dead near the generator are put down before each held job (they
# would break it off), after the Sergeant has been met.
# Run in a fresh directory (it writes saves):
#   dead_pixel_survival.exe --script tools/harness/gate_across_lives.txt --hidden --world-seed 1234 --shots shots/gate_across_lives
