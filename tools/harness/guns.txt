# Guns (phase 3): a pistol loaded from the pack with R, a shot that drops one
# of the dead and is heard far off, the empty click, then the shotgun loaded
# a shell at a time and its spread that puts the close ones on the floor.
# Run in a fresh directory:
#   dead_pixel_survival.exe --script tools/harness/guns.txt --hidden --world-seed 1234 --shots shots/guns

newrun
god on
waits 1
# Open grass, so a shot at six tiles has a clear line.
teleport 62 66
waits 0.3
zoom 2.5
mouse 760 420

# The pistol comes empty; R loads it from the rounds in the pack.
give Pistol
give Pistol Ammo 9
select Pistol
expect loaded 0
waits 0.2
shot pistol_empty_prompt
reload
waits 2
expect loaded 8
shot pistol_loaded

# A shot at six tiles: the line, the flash, the case; it kills the one in
# front (50 health), costs one round, and every wanderer in earshot comes to
# look. (A Loud noise reaches 30 tiles; since phase 4 it carries 20 % less
# upwind and a Brute hears at 0.7, so every kind is sure to hear it within
# about 16.8 tiles.) It stands still: an idle one drifts a few pixels before
# the shot, and how far depends on every roll the town took before it.
zombie 6 0 still hp 50
# A wanderer well inside earshot, behind the shooter.
zombie -14 0 shambler
waits 0.2
click left
wait 8
shot pistol_shot
waits 0.4
expect loaded 7
expect kills 1
zombies
expect zombies all 16
slay 40
set panic 0
waits 0.5

# Seven more, then the empty click.
click left
waits 0.6
click left
waits 0.6
click left
waits 0.6
click left
waits 0.6
click left
waits 0.6
click left
waits 0.6
click left
waits 0.6
expect loaded 0
click left
waits 0.2
shot pistol_dry
reload
waits 2
expect loaded 1

# The shotgun: two shells, one at a time.
give Shotgun
give Shotgun Shells 6
select Shotgun
reload
waits 2.2
expect loaded 2
shot shotgun_loaded
# Two side by side at a tile and a half: the spread drops both. (Pellets
# stop at the first body, so two in a line would shield each other. The
# kill count includes the seven slain above: slay counts as yours.)
expect kills 8
zombie 1.5 0.28 idle exact
zombie 1.5 -0.28 idle exact
waits 0.1
click left
wait 13
shot shotgun_blast
waits 0.3
expect loaded 1
expect kills 10
shot shotgun_down
slay 40
waits 1
expect alive
quit
