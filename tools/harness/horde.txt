# A horde (phase 4): on a horde night the crowd crosses the town past the
# house. Lights out and the house shut, it passes by and none come in. With
# a candle in an uncurtained window, some turn to it and break in.
# The town's people are taken away first: since phase 9 (the gate changes
# the town) the horde set out near a lived-in house, and its evening lamp
# drew every one of them in before they reached this one.
# Run in a fresh directory:
#   dead_pixel_survival.exe --script tools/harness/horde.txt --hidden --world-seed 1234 --shots shots/horde

mode mutating
newrun
god on
waits 1
survivors gone
zoom 1.5
day 7
hours 20.9
slay 200
goto home
# Shut up: every window curtained, no light.
window curtained all
waits 0.3
horde
expect horde on
waits 30
shot horde_passing
waits 300
expect zombies outside 60
shot horde_passed_by
# Dawn: it has gone.
hours 7
waits 2
expect horde off
expect zombies outside 60

# The next night, another horde, and a candle burning in a clear window.
hours 20.9
slay 200
goto home
window curtained all
goto window home
window open
expect window clear
give Candle
give Matches
waits 0.2
tap 5
tap F
waits 0.3
light
waits 0.3
horde
expect horde on
waits 30
shot horde_drawn_to_light
waits 300
expect zombies inside 60
shot horde_broke_in
quit
