# Journal (phase 10): a scripted life with a notebook - the first kill,
# boards on a door, a bite, the first night, death on day 3 - writes six to
# nine entries. The next life reads them at the grave.
# Run in a fresh directory (it writes saves):
#   dead_pixel_survival.exe --script tools/harness/journal.txt --hidden --world-seed 1234 --shots shots/journal

survivors gone
newrun
god on
waits 1
give Notebook
waits 0.5
# The first kill.
zombie 2 0
waits 0.3
slay 4
waits 0.5
# Boards across the door.
goto door
waits 0.3
give Hammer
give Wood Planks 3
give Nails 6
tap R
waits 4.5
# Bitten.
wound bite larm
waits 0.3
# Through the first night.
day 2
hours 7
waits 0.5
day 3
hours 9
waits 0.5
journal
expect journal 5 8
tap J
tap Left
waits 0.3
shot 01_journal_tab
tap J
kill Bled out
waits 0.5
journal last
expect journal last 6 9

# The next life finds the grave and reads it.
newrun
god on
waits 1
goto grave
waits 0.3
expect prompt E: Read
tap E
waits 0.4
expect reader open
shot 02_reader_at_grave
tap Down
waits 0.2
tap J
waits 0.2
expect reader closed
# The notebook's "Before you" page: Enter reads it again.
tap J
waits 0.1
tap Left
waits 0.1
tap Left
waits 0.1
tap Left
waits 0.2
shot 03_before_you
tap Return
waits 0.3
expect reader open
shot 04_reader_from_notebook
tap J
quit
