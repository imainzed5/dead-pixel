# Power (phase 6): on day 5 near dusk the town's power fails. "The power is
# out."; the street lamps go dark; a fridge stops keeping food (half speed
# while it runs, full speed after); a floor lamp lights a room only while
# there is power.
# Run in a fresh directory:
#   dead_pixel_survival.exe --script tools/harness/power_out.txt --hidden --world-seed 1234 --shots shots/power_out

newrun
god on
waits 1
slay 300
# The clock just short of this run's failure (day 5, near dusk give or
# take 12 hours): it goes by itself.
power soon
expect power on
waits 10
expect power off
expect message power is out
# Day 5, after dark, by a street lamp: lit while the power is held on, dark
# when it goes.
hours 21
lamp
zoom 2
power on
waits 1.5
shot 01_lamps_lit
power auto
waits 1.5
expect power off
shot 02_lamps_dark
# A fridge: half speed while it runs (two game hours), full speed after.
slay 300
goto furniture fridge
power on
fridge mark
waits 120
expect fridgerate 0.35 0.65
power off
fridge mark
waits 120
expect fridgerate 0.85 1.15
# A floor lamp, switched on: a lit room while the power is held on.
goto furniture floorlamp
power on
tap E
waits 1.5
shot 03_floor_lamp_on
power off
waits 1.5
shot 04_floor_lamp_dead
expect alive
quit
