# District ruin (phase 4): the district's ruin level from its infection shows
# on the street (litter, smears on walls, bodies), as a word on the HUD
# ("quiet", "uneasy", "turning", "overrun") and as shading on the town map;
# and a bleeding player leaves drops of blood on the ground.
# Run in a fresh directory:
#   dead_pixel_survival.exe --script tools/harness/ruin.txt --hidden --world-seed 1234 --shots shots/ruin

newrun
god on
waits 1
zoom 1.5
slay 60
infection 10
waits 0.5
shot ruin_quiet
infection 30 all
infection 80
waits 0.5
shot ruin_overrun
tap M
waits 0.3
shot ruin_map
tap M
waits 0.3
zoom 2.5
goto pavement
waits 0.3
set bleeding 30
hold D 240
waits 4.5
waits 0.5
expect trail 3
shot blood_drops
quit
