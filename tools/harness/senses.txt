# How the dead sense you (phase 4): sight in the dark and crouching, noise
# through the walls of a shut house, rain muffling footsteps, and a blood
# trail that a zombie follows (and rain and a bandage that end it).
# Run in a fresh directory:
#   dead_pixel_survival.exe --script tools/harness/senses.txt --hidden --world-seed 1234 --shots shots/senses

mode stalker
newrun
god on
# No bad trait: a Clumsy player's steps are a tier louder.
trait plain
waits 1
zoom 2.5
slay 80

# In the dark, crouched and still, 5 tiles in front of a Shambler: not seen
# for 10 s. Running past it, it comes.
hours 23
lamp
hold D 12
# The steps die away before it is put there.
waits 3
zombie 0 -5 shambler still
hold C 600
waits 10
expect kind shambler calm 7
shot dark_crouched_unseen
hold D 40
hold LSHIFT 40
waits 1.5
expect kind shambler coming 15
shot running_heard
slay 20
waits 0.5

# A swing indoors (a Medium noise) with every door and window shut is not
# heard 10 tiles outside; with the door open it is.
hours 12
goto home
waits 0.3
zombie outside 10 shambler still
noise medium
waits 2
expect kind shambler calm 12
goto door
tap E
waits 0.3
goto home
waits 0.3
noise medium
waits 2
expect kind shambler coming 12
shot heard_through_open_door
slay 20
waits 0.5

# In heavy rain a walking player is not heard 5 tiles off; in clear weather
# the same walk is. (At night, and it faces away: only its ears count. On
# ordinary pavement: a road's hard ground makes steps a tier louder.)
hours 23
goto pavement
waits 3
weather heavyrain
waits 0.5
zombie 0 5 shambler still
hold D 20
waits 0.5
hold A 20
waits 0.5
hold D 20
waits 0.5
hold A 20
waits 1
expect kind shambler calm 7
shot rain_unheard
weather clear
hold D 20
waits 0.5
hold A 20
waits 1
expect kind shambler coming 7
slay 20
waits 0.5

# Bleeding, the player walks 15 tiles and stops; a Shambler put where the
# walk began follows the blood and arrives within 60 s.
lamp
waits 0.3
mark
set bleeding 60
hold D 160
waits 3
set bleeding 0
trail
expect trail 6
zombie mark shambler still
waits 2
shot trail_followed
waits 58
expect kind shambler near 3
shot trail_arrived
slay 30
waits 0.5

# With the bleeding stopped and the wounds dressed (a bandage) and rain,
# the same walk leaves nothing, and the one at the start stays there.
weather rain
set wounds 0
# The rain washes the last of the old trail away first.
waits 11
lamp
waits 0.3
mark
hold D 160
waits 3
expect trail none
zombie mark shambler still
waits 60
expect kind shambler far 6
shot trail_none
quit
