# Survivors (phase 8): the other people in town live by their own needs.
# One with little in the house goes out to a cupboard, searches it, comes
# home with the food (the cupboard is remembered as emptied) and eats when
# hungry; one caught out by three of the dead runs home and shuts the door;
# one with planks boards a door. Then the days between lives, twice the
# same for one seed, then for real.
# Run in a fresh directory (it writes saves):
#   dead_pixel_survival.exe --script tools/harness/survivors.txt --hidden --world-seed 1234 --shots shots/survivors

newrun
god on
hours 9
waits 1
# The town's dead out of the way: only the ones the script puts down.
slay 200
survivors

# Low stock: out to the cupboard they would choose (a few cans put in it).
# Trust 30: the player may walk into the house to look without a fight.
survivor house open low trust 30
survivor fill Canned Food 3
waits 3
expect survivor scavenge
goto survivor
waits 0.5
shot 01_going_out
waitfor 90 survivor searched
expect survivor stock Canned Food 4
waitfor 90 survivor home
waits 1
expect survivor stock Canned Food 4
goto survivor
waits 0.5
shot 02_home_with_it

# Hungry now: they eat from what they keep.
set shunger 30
waitfor 10 survivor hunger above 45
expect survivor stock Canned Food 3
survivors

# Caught out: three of the dead close by; they run home and shut the door.
survivor house open low trust 30
survivor fill Canned Food 1
waitfor 30 survivor away
waits 2
goto survivor
survivor dead 3
waitfor 3 survivor flee
waits 0.3
shot 03_running_home
waitfor 40 survivor home
waitfor 10 survivor shut
waits 5
expect survivor home
expect survivor shut
goto survivor
waits 0.5
shot 04_shut_in
slay 40

# Planks and nails: a door boarded (they keep the other to come and go).
# Few houses have two doors; on this seed the nearest free one is a warehouse.
survivor house open planks doors2 trust 30
goto survivor
waitfor 60 survivor boarded door
waits 0.5
shot 05_boarded
survivors

# Between lives: ten days, twice the same; then for real.
advance 10
advance 10
expect advance same
advance 10 apply
expect advance loots 1
survivors
quit
