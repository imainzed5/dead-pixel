# Buildings with a purpose (phase 6): the town has its pharmacy, grocery,
# hardware store, clinic, police station and garage; each wears a sign over
# its street door; walking past marks it on the town map, for good.
# Run on seeds 1234, 42 and 777, each in a fresh directory:
#   dead_pixel_survival.exe --script tools/harness/town_kinds.txt --hidden --world-seed 1234 --shots shots/town_kinds

newrun
god on
waits 1
slay 300
hours 12
locks
# Each kind's street front, from three tiles out, at the default zoom.
goto kind pharmacy 3
waits 1.5
shot 01_pharmacy_front
goto kind grocery 3
waits 1.5
shot 02_grocery_front
goto kind hardware 3
waits 1.5
shot 03_hardware_front
goto kind clinic 3
waits 1.5
shot 04_clinic_front
goto kind police 3
waits 1.5
shot 05_police_front
goto kind garage 3
waits 1.5
shot 06_garage_front
# Inside: shelves, cots, lockers, racks.
goto kind grocery inside
waits 1.5
shot 07_grocery_inside
goto kind police inside
waits 1.5
shot 08_police_inside
goto kind clinic inside
waits 1.5
shot 09_clinic_inside
# The town map shows the six kinds just walked past.
tap M
waits 0.5
shot 10_map_seen
tap M
waits 0.3
expect alive
quit
