# Trade (phase 8): the talk panel and the trade panel, at 1280x720. A
# survivor who is well wants more than one can for two bandages; hungry,
# the same can is worth twice as much to them, they take it and trust you
# more for it. Then the notebook's page of people and the map's homes.
# Run in a fresh directory (it writes saves):
#   dead_pixel_survival.exe --script tools/harness/trade.txt --hidden --world-seed 1234 --shots shots/trade

newrun
god on
hours 10
waits 1
slay 200
give Canned Food 2

# Someone open, in the house next door, with bandages to spare.
survivor house open bandages trust 20
goto survivor
waits 1
talk
waits 0.3
expect talk open
shot 01_talk_panel
talk hello
expect survivor trust above 22
waits 0.3
shot 02_said_hello

# Well fed: a can is worth 10 to them, two bandages 16. They want more.
set shunger 90
talk trade
expect talk trade
trade offer Canned Food 1
trade ask Bandage 2
waits 0.3
shot 03_trade_panel
trade agree
expect talk trade
expect survivor stock Bandage 3
expect survivor trust below 24

# Hungry: food counts twice. Fair, and they take it.
set shunger 30
trade offer Canned Food 1
trade ask Bandage 2
waits 0.3
shot 04_trade_hungry
trade agree
expect survivor stock Bandage 1
expect survivor trust above 27
expect pack Bandage 2
waits 0.3
shot 05_traded

# Tab back to the talk view, Tab again to close.
tap TAB
waits 0.2
expect talk open
tap TAB
waits 0.2
expect talk closed

# The notebook lists who you met; the map marks the homes you know.
tap J
waits 0.3
shot 06_notebook
tap J
tap M
waits 0.3
shot 07_map
tap M
quit
